💡 How Your Match Scores Were Calculated

Our recommendation system employs a multi-factor weighting algorithm:

  • Academic & Stream Affinity (~40%): Evaluates your current branch, percentage/CGPA, and directly scores careers matching your favorite academic subjects, existing skills, and preferred fields.
  • Situational Aptitude Assessment (~60%): Evaluates your answers across 8 specialized domains (Programming, AI, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Electronics, Business, UX Design, and Communication).
  • Normalization & Ranking: Scores are dynamically scaled into an intuitive 45% - 98% percentage range and ranked to highlight your highest-potential pathway.
